Manual answer review rubric
  1. Can each factual claim be supported by an inspected passage?
  2. Do the cited sources actually support the claim, rather than merely sharing its topic?
  3. Does an answer acknowledge missing or contradictory evidence?
  4. Did any retrieval gain justify the measured latency and longer prompt?
